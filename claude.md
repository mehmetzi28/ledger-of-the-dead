Ledger of the Dead: single-file HTML/CSS/JS necromancer RPG.
- index.html is the shipped game, generated from src/ and committed; never edit it by hand. Edit files in `src/`, then run `node tools/build.js` (each `@@include` line in the template becomes that file's exact bytes). `node tools/build.js --check` fails if index.html differs from src/ (run-tests.js and CI run it first). serve.js rebuilds automatically when a src file is newer, so edit and refresh.
- src/: `index.template.html` (page shell and markup), `style.css` (all CSS); game logic, in this order: `game-data.js` (ranks, gates, roster, secret bosses, items, drop tables, gear rolls, Hell Tower, the DIFF block), `game-player.js` (player stats, new game, backups, save slots and codes, normalizeSave, titles), `game-runs.js` (dungeon runs, combat, bag, raising, the defeat recap and Instant Retry), `game-skills-market.js` (skills, gold and the market, minion traits and death traits, fusion-only compound traits (COMPOUNDS), evolution marks, raids, the Rift, tips), `game-gate-rules.js` (boss abilities, status effects, foe intents and bestiary knowledge on them, corpse conditions, events, cursed gates, omens, gate difficulty and its rules (HEAT), history, records, doors (each door pre-rolls its room, shown as far as your Ledger knowledge goes), gear sets), `game-faith-story.js` (faith, skill upgrades, minion commands, story and boss voices, torn pages), `game-phases-town.js` (boss phases, elite affixes, town buildings and gate-clear unlocks (OPEN_AT), the Reserve, banked bounties, ultimates, fusion, item locks, salvage, enchanting and tempering, siege nights (with a guard building and Reserve minions on the wall), the Memorial, loadouts), `game-rival-trials.js` (the Rival, who fields the bodies they stole, and weekly Trials), `game-progression.js` (mastery paths, capstones, New Game+ pacts); then UI: `scenes.js` (from the `//==UI==` marker: sound, music, painted backdrops, boss arenas, display settings), `fx.js` (the battle effects layer and the battle stage: fighters drawn from their icons on the painted scene, read from state after each render; draws only, no game rules; off without PixiJS, on Light effects or reduced motion, where the battle shows its cards), `tr.js` (the Turkish tables `TR_DICT` and `TR_RX`), `ui-view.js` (Turkish lookup, view settings, save files), `ui-icons.js` (all icons and painted bosses), `ui-panels.js` (item cards, gear, bag, bestiary, status, gates, titles, tower, market, skills, help, saves, lore, records, history, the Memorial, loadouts), `ui-codex.js` (the Codex, faith and buildings panels, What next and its Next objective), `ui-rival-trials.js` (the rival's icon and arena, the Trial card, Rival and Trial records, their Codex pages), `ui-progression.js` (Skills mastery paths, New Game+ pact picker), `ui-screens.js` (town, battle, army, raise, doors, events, spoils, defeat and its recap, shortcuts), `ui-input.js` (render, actions, input, startup). Game logic goes in the game-*.js files; UI and icons in scenes.js and the files after it. The build joins them in template order, so a top-level statement never spans two files.
- Saves use localStorage key `ledger-of-the-dead-v1`. Keep loadGame backward compatible: every load goes through normalizeSave (repairs/migrates) and applyLoaded.
- Browsers block localStorage on data: pages, so test saves with `node tools/serve.js` (http://localhost:5173, launch config "game").
- Difficulty is tuned with the DIFF block (gates), HEAT (Hard/Brutal/Nightmare), the TW constants (Hell Tower), SIEGE_* (SIEGE_WALL: Reserve minions on the wall), RIVAL_* (RIVAL_STASH: stolen bodies the rival keeps), the temper cost (TEMPER_GOLD/TEMPER_GROW, game-phases-town.js), the headstone constants (HEAD_*, game-phases-town.js), Guard's SB_TAKE (game-faith-story.js), COMPOUNDS (game-skills-market.js), the capstone constants at the top of src/game-progression.js, the bond and mausoleum constants, CORPSE_CONDS and the death-trait constants (game-gate-rules.js) and MARK (game-skills-market.js). Balance was tested with bots only. The bot lives in tests/balance-test.js: paste it into the browser console with the game open, or load it from the local server (see the top of the file), then run LOTD_BALANCE.run({modes:['market']}). Faster: `node tests/run-balance.js` (options as JSON, e.g. '{"seed":9100}'). Measured 2026-11-20 (seeds 7700/9100) after the difficulty rules (Hard doubles elites, Brutal affixes the front row and moves the boss's second phase to 65%, Nightmare puts an elite in every room and starts bosses in phase two), corpse conditions and death traits, evolution marks, bestiary second intents and gate-clear unlocks (HEAT foe Hard 1.8, Brutal 2.5, Nightmare 3.6; Hard XP 1.3->1.2, as doubled elites pay more; marks Spiked/Marksman .15->.1, Volley/Warden .3->.2, Butcher .2->.15; rival 12.8/5.4->14.8/6.2), and again the same day after the rival fields stolen bodies, tempering, fusion compounds, the siege wall and pre-rolled doors (rival 14.8/6.2->15.4/6.4, TEMPER_GOLD 60->150): 'market' 87/88% (gates 78-89%, the Rat Warren 93-94%), Hell Tower ~70-71, Rift average deepest ~15-17 (15.4 after the combat fixes below), raises answered ~20% of attempts; the S gate is farmed ~15 levels over and its losses are the hero one-shot in the back row, so foe stats barely move it (S foeAtk 1.55->2.2 or DIFF.gate 2.45->2.8 moved it under 2 points while shifting its Recommended level by several), and market sits ~2 over the ~85 target for that reason. The 'full' mode (`'{"modes":["market","full"]}'`) also uses buildings, salvage, enchanting, fusion, bounties, orders and mausoleums and should land 1-3 points above 'market': 89/88%, Tower ~82-84, Rift ~8-9, raises ~32%; it swears faith and plays Hard on cleared gates by default; with `"heat":0` (Normal only) 92% (seed 7700), the limit. `"stats":["will","constitution"]` replaces the bot's stat order (market, seed 7700: Will+Constitution 89%, Will+Strength 88%, Will+Intelligence 88%, Will 85%, within ~4 points); `"mods":"a"|"b"` buys every skill's rank-3 upgrade a or b (default none, so earlier numbers stay comparable), `"mod":"mend:a"` rushes that one skill to rank 3 and takes that upgrade. `"off":["fuse"]` switches parts off to measure them (also `maus` for mausoleums, `bonds` for minion bonds, `marks` for evolution marks: about 1 point of clears, ~2 Rift depths). `faith`, `ult` and `heat` options measure the oath, the ultimate and gate difficulty; `LOTD_BALANCE.duel` tests secret masters; the full bot also orders Commander raids (about 78% won; before the day's last run; a raid is an order for tonight: the squad fights the rest of the day and marches when the day ends, keeps its army or Reserve places overnight so a siege raise cannot fill them, and steps back in at dawn; only if those places are gone does it fall back to the Reserve or the gate), uses the Reserve (benches bodies once a gate is cleared and swaps in whoever beats its weakest minion), raises mausoleums with spare gold (about 4 of 5 by day 100), dives the Rift at the end (reports average and best depth) and learns the six small passives; fights siege nights like rooms (reports sieges fought and held; 80-87% held, 83% after the 2026-11-20 combat fixes: boss adds now scale with difficulty and the Rift, siege waves start like rooms), `"off":["raid"]`, `["rift"]`, `["reserve"]` or `["siege"]` measures without them; the Rival plays only in 'full' (so 'market' is unchanged): the bot meets each challenge as a boss room (about 68% won, 68/68% now), challenge runs stay out of the gate table, and `"off":["rival"]` measures without thefts and challenges. The full bot also tempers worn gear while it holds over 10000 gold (`"off":["temper"]` measures without it). Gold: at day 100 the bots hold avg/median ~33-35k/15-20k (market) and ~34-39k/22-28k (full); late income (~1.1M lifetime in full) outruns every capped sink: tempering stops once every bonus line on the three gear slots is at its best (uniques cannot be tempered), so a higher cost or growth barely moves it, and mausoleums do not fully absorb it. The 'full' bot plays events and event chains with a simple policy (`"off":["events"]`: about 1 point of clears, 4 Tower floors), carves headstones with spare gold above 10000 (`"off":["head"]`: gold held at day 100 ~20-22k instead of ~39k) and gives the Guard order before a telegraphed move that would kill the hero, or under 25% HP when a visible hit would (`"off":["guard"]`), and plays events in the Rift too; 'market' uses none of them. Nightmare's Recommended level was re-probed the same day (REC_TIER .4 -> .18; the bots reach ~80% from D 10, C 18, B 24-26), so it now sits one level above Brutal's. New Game+ pacts only exist in NG+. `LOTD_HTML=path` makes the Node runners load a tuned copy of index.html. `node tools/perf.js` prints rough render timings in Node with the tests' fake browser.
- Tests: `node tests/run-tests.js` runs 655 tests (one file per system in tests/groups/, helpers and runner in tests/game-tests.js, plus a 30-day bot playthrough) against the built index.html in Node with a fake browser; add `market` etc. to run matching tests. Run it after every change. It first checks the build, then runs tests/lint.js on src/*.js, which fails if a `//` comment was dropped into the middle of a one-line statement and swallowed the code after it (put mid-line notes in `/* */` or at the end of the line), or if a stray control character got into a src file. In the browser: load tests/game-tests.js, then `await LOTD_TESTS.load(); LOTD_TESTS.run()`. GitHub Actions runs it on every push. A second server for play-testing with separate saves: `node tools/serve.js 5174` (launch config "playtest"); a third, "scratch" on 5175, is for throwaway visual checks that must not touch either save (a tab on the same port as a game in use makes that game stop saving).
- Fuzzing: `node tests/fuzz.js` clicks random buttons for thousands of steps per seed and checks the same rules after every click; run it after big changes. Don't edit or rebuild index.html while it runs, because each seed reloads the file.
- Dark palette. Blue and purple are only for the Rare and Epic item tiers, with one exception the owner chose: mana is blue (its glyphs and the mana bar). Look: worn and textured, not cartoonish (the "Grim realism pass" CSS block; icons are painted through the #paint SVG filter, coloured by MAT (natural colour and material per creature, rank only on the frame) and textured by the tx* patterns that P() lays on every shape; bosses and the Necromancer are hand-detailed with RIM/FOLD); no italic fonts.
- Turkish: Settings > Language. English is written into the page and `trDom` lays Turkish over it after each render from the `TR_DICT` table (numbers as #, keys without outer punctuation). New UI text needs a Turkish entry there; text with names in it uses a pattern in `TR_RX` (names stay English, $n keeps a group, {tn} translates it). Boss speech is translated through a pattern too.
- Libraries only as vendored files in lib/ (now PixiJS 8.23.0, for the battle effects layer in src/fx.js, loaded at runtime); no CDNs except Google Fonts. The game and the tests must work with the library absent.
- Line endings are LF everywhere (.gitattributes). Write files with LF.
